This policy describes how Atelier Fichier and its assistant process your data. We follow a simple principle: collect only what is strictly necessary for the service to work.

1. Data controller

The Atelier Fichier service is published by its author, who can be reached through the project page on GitHub: github.com/regemathieu-hue/atelier-fichier.

2. Data processed

  • Content of your exchanges with the assistant: the messages you send are processed only to generate a reply.
  • Files: the files you submit are processed only to carry out the requested operation. They are not kept once the operation is complete.
  • Technical data: minimal technical information (browser type, timestamp, errors) may be recorded to keep the service running and secure.

3. Purposes

  • Provide the service and respond to your requests.
  • Ensure security, detect abuse and fix errors.
  • Improve the service, using only aggregated and anonymized data.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your files or conversations for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with third parties, except technical providers strictly required to run the service or where the law requires it.

5. Retention period

  • Submitted files: deleted as soon as processing is complete.
  • Conversations with the assistant: not kept beyond the session, except for technical logs.
  • Technical logs: kept for a maximum of 30 days.

6. Processors

To produce its replies, the assistant may rely on a language model provider. The data sent is limited to what is needed for the reply and is covered by that provider's confidentiality commitments.

7. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your data. To exercise these rights, open a ticket on the project's issue tracker on GitHub. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Cookies

These pages use no cookies or trackers.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date of the latest update appears at the top of this page.
