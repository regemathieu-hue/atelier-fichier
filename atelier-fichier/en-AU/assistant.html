What is the assistant?

The Atelier Fichier assistant helps you prepare, organise and convert your files. You describe what you want to achieve and it guides you step by step.

What the assistant can do

  • Answer your questions about preparing and formatting your files.
  • Suggest a procedure tailored to your needs.
  • Point you to the right resource when it cannot handle the request.

What the assistant does not do

  • It does not make decisions for you: you stay in control of your files.
  • It does not keep your conversations beyond what is described in the privacy policy.
  • It is not a substitute for professional advice (legal, accounting or technical).
